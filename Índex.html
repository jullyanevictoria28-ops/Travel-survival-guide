🇫🇷 Paris

The adventure begins.

The girls arrive at Charles de Gaulle Airport excited to start their European adventure. But the trip immediately gives them a little challenge: they are running late for their next flight, their hotel room is not ready yet, and one of them gets separated from the group while admiring a shop window.

hurry up
Phrasal Verb — to move quickly.
They have to hurry up to catch their flight.
wait for
Prepositional Verb — to stay until someone or something arrives.
They wait for their hotel room in the lobby.
get away from
Phrasal-Prepositional Verb — to move away from someone or something.
One of the girls gets away from the group while looking at a shop window.

🇮🇹 Rome

When the city has a story for you.

After arriving in Rome, the girls try a traditional Italian restaurant and discover that understanding a menu in another language can be an adventure of its own. Later, they take a wrong street while searching for a store and stop to ask a friendly — and very handsome — local man for directions.

At the store, the excitement continues until they realize they are running out of money. They laugh, check their budget and promise to be a little more careful for the rest of the trip.

figure out
Phrasal Verb — to understand or discover.
They figure out which dishes they want to order.
ask for
Prepositional Verb — to request.
They ask for directions.
run out of
Phrasal-Prepositional Verb — to have no more of something.
They realize they are running out of money.

🇪🇸 Barcelona

Music, sunshine and a little detour.

On their first evening in Barcelona, the girls watch a lively Spanish dance performance. The music is so energetic that one of them cannot resist and decides to join in with the dancers for a few seconds. Everyone laughs — and the night becomes one of their favorite memories.

The next day brings sunshine, the beach and a small detour. They ask about places to visit nearby, then realize Natália has stopped to take pictures. The girls turn around, catch up with her and continue the afternoon together.

join in
Phrasal Verb — to participate or take part.
One of the girls joins in with the dancers.
ask about
Prepositional Verb — to ask for information about something.
They ask about places to visit nearby.
catch up with
Phrasal-Prepositional Verb — to reach someone who is ahead.
The girls catch up with Natália.

🇨🇭 Zurich

A slower ending to a busy adventure.

After several busy days, the girls decide to slow down. They take a train toward the mountains, walk through the peaceful landscape and spend time simply talking and taking pictures.

Later, they find a cozy café and talk about everything they have experienced. On their last evening, they sit by the lake, watch the sunset and look back on the journey. The little problems they faced have become some of their favorite memories.

slow down
Phrasal Verb — to become less fast.
They decide to slow down and enjoy the landscape.
talk about
Prepositional Verb — to discuss something.
They talk about everything they have experienced.
look back on
Phrasal-Prepositional Verb — to remember and reflect on the past.
They look back on the journey.

After the Journey

“Some trips end at the airport. Others stay with us forever.”

A few days later, the girls were back home, surrounded by their usual routines again. But something had changed. They had returned with hundreds of photos, funny stories and memories that would probably make them laugh for years.

They still talked about the missed turns, the unexpected moments, the beautiful places and all the little things that happened along the way. What seemed like problems during the trip had become some of their favorite memories.

They realized that traveling was not only about visiting new countries or taking beautiful pictures. It was about discovering new places, learning from different experiences and, most importantly, sharing those moments with people who make life more special.

The trip was over, but the memories were just beginning.
